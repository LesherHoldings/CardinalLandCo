About Cardinal Land Company

A Pennsylvania firm. A straight answer.

We assess land for landowners across all of Pennsylvania and introduce them to vetted energy and land companies. The assessment is free, and the companies pay our fee.

Joe Lesher, Principal

Joe Lesher has spent more than ten years helping Pennsylvania homeowners and landowners. He is an Army veteran with roots in Lancaster County, where the firm keeps its office today.

He founded Cardinal Land Company after seeing the same thing happen in county after county. A letter arrives from a land agent. It names one use for the land, usually the one the agent is selling, and asks for an answer. The landowner has no one to ask what the land could earn across every use, only people with something to sell.

That is the gap this firm fills. We assess land for solar, natural gas, minerals, and timber. We explain what we find in plain words. Where a vetted company is a genuine fit, we introduce you, and the company pays our fee. It is plain work, done carefully. We serve landowners in every part of Pennsylvania, and the companies we introduce come from across the country.

How we work

Four rules we keep

They are the same for a ten-acre woodlot as for a thousand-acre farm, and the same in every county in the state.

  • We assess every use, not just the one in the letter

    A solar letter does not mean solar is the best use of your ground, or the only one. Every assessment looks at solar, natural gas, minerals, and timber.

  • We tell you if nothing fits

    Some land is not a match for any of these right now. When that is the case we say so plainly, the assessment is still free, and there is no bill later.

  • We introduce, we do not represent

    We are not your agent and we do not negotiate for you. You keep your own attorney, you read every page, and you make your own decision. Our job is to explain what we found and put a vetted company in front of you.

  • Your information stays in our office

    Nothing about you or your land goes to an energy or land company without your say-so.

Plain speaking

What we are not

It helps to be clear about this.

  • We are not a solar company, a gas company, or a timber buyer. We do not lease or buy land ourselves.
  • We are not a law firm. We do not give legal advice, and we do not review or approve leases. A second opinion from us is about your land and what else it could do, not about the legal terms of an offer. Your own attorney should read any agreement before you sign it.
  • We are not appraisers or financial advisers. Our assessment tells you which uses your land may fit and who is active in your part of the state. It is not an appraisal, and it is not financial advice.
  • We are paid by the companies when a deal closes, and nothing else. There is no retainer and no fee to you.
