Four ways your land can pay you

Many landowners hear about only one of these, usually from whoever sent the letter. We look at all four.

  • Solar Leasing

    Solar companies lease open ground near power lines and pay rent by the acre, often for 25 years or more. We check whether your fields fit what they are looking for before you talk to anyone.

    About solar leases

  • Natural Gas

    If you own the gas under your land, a company may pay a bonus to lease it and a royalty on what it produces. We help you find out what you own and which companies are active in your part of the state.

    About gas leases

  • Mineral Rights

    The rights under your land can be leased or sold on their own, and many families are not sure what they hold. We check what the county record shows you hold, and who is buying or leasing in your county.

    About mineral rights

  • Timber

    Standing timber is a crop, and it is worth knowing what is in your woods before a logger knocks on the door. We introduce you to reputable buyers and foresters before anything is cut.

    About timber sales
